\item[4.1.34] Solve $ye^y\,dx=(y^3+2xe^y)\,dy$, given $y(0)=1$.

\vspace{0.6em}\par\solution


Treat $x$ as a function of $y$. Dividing by $ye^y$ gives $\frac{dx}{dy}-\frac{2x}{y}=y^2e^{-y}$. The integrating factor is $y^{-2}$, so $\frac{d}{dy}(x/y^2)=e^{-y}$.
